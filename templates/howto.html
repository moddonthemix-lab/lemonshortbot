  • Landing preloads data. When you open any tab, cached results render instantly.
  • Use the Refresh button only when you want a fresh scan—previous results stay visible while it works.
  • Short Squeeze defaults: Min Short 25%, Gain 15%, Vol Ratio 1.5x, Risk 60. Tune and refresh.
  • Crypto / Hourly / Daily / Weekly already load top patterns; news is limited to the freshest links.
  • LemonAI auto-refreshes hourly; caches are kept for fast page loads.
